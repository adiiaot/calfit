# CalFit — Speaker Notes (about 5 minutes total)

## Opening (0:00–0:30)
"Good morning. I am [INSERT NAME]. My project is CalFit, one app for tracking food, workouts, sleep and fasting, with an AI coach. I will show you the problem I found, how the app solves it, and how I tested my work."

## Problem (0:30–1:15)
"People don't quit fitness because they are lazy. They quit because their records are scattered, their food isn't in foreign apps, every plan looks the same, and nobody checks in. When you can't see progress, you stop trying."

## Solution + features (1:15–2:30)
"CalFit answers each cause: everything in one place, local dishes plus a photo scanner, an AI coach that knows your history, and partners, streaks and reminders that nudge you daily."
Then walk through the three screenshots, one sentence each. Transition: "Let me briefly show how it is built."

## How it is built (2:30–3:15)
"One codebase for Android and iPhone. Your records sit in your private online account. The AI is reached through a secure middleman so secret keys never hide inside the app." Transition: "Here is proof the work was tested."

## Testing (3:15–4:15)
Mention two or three fixes by name — the launch crash, the retired AI model, the fasting timer — then the numbers: 32 automatic checks pass, 21 out of 21 health checks pass. Add honestly: "Phone testing is still in progress, and my report says so." Transition: "Finally, what I learned."

## Lessons + close (4:15–5:00)
"Verify field names after database changes. Keep AI model names replaceable. Never let the app crash silently." Then: "Before this project I already had software planning and coding experience; during my IT I additionally learned freelancing, negotiation, pricing and budgeting. AI tools helped me draft code, but every line entered the project only after my review and a passing test. Thank you — I welcome your questions."
