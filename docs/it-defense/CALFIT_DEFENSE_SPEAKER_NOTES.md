# CalFit — Speaker Notes (about 5 minutes total)

## Opening (0:00–0:30)
"Good morning. I'm Oluwadare Taye Ayo. My project is CalFit — one app for tracking food, workouts, sleep and fasting, with an AI coach. I'll tell you the problem I found, what I built to solve it, and how I proved my work."

## The problem (0:30–1:15)
"I kept watching people download fitness apps in January and delete them months later. It wasn't laziness. Their records were scattered, their food wasn't in these foreign apps, every plan looked the same, and nobody checked in. My one-line summary is: you can't stay motivated by progress you can't see."

## The solution and features (1:15–2:30)
"So I built CalFit to answer each cause directly — everything in one place, local dishes plus a photo scanner, an AI coach that knows your history, and partners, streaks and reminders for the daily nudge."
Walk through the three screenshots, one sentence each. Then: "Let me quickly show you how it's put together."

## How it's built (2:30–3:15)
"One codebase for both Android and iPhone. Your records sit in your private online account. The AI is reached through a secure middleman, so the secret keys never hide inside the app." Then: "Now the part I care about most — the proof."

## Testing (3:15–4:15)
Pick your two favourite war stories — I'd suggest the launch crash in front of the assessor and the AI model dying overnight. Then the numbers: 32 checks pass, 21 out of 21 health checks pass. And say it with a straight face: "Phone testing is still ongoing, and my report says exactly what's done and what isn't." Examiners respect that line more than any perfect score. Then: "Finally, what this taught me."

## Lessons and close (4:15–5:00)
"Verify field names after database changes. Keep AI models replaceable. Never let an app crash silently." Then the background line: "I came in with software experience, which I used to plan and review this work. My IT placement added freelancing, negotiation, pricing and budgeting on top." And the AI line: "AI tools helped me draft and debug, but every line entered this project only after my review and a passing test — if it broke, it was my call." Thank them. Take questions.
