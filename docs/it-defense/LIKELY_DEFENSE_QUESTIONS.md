# CalFit — Likely Defence Questions (how I'd actually answer)

1. **What problem did you find?** "People quit fitness within weeks — not from laziness, but because their records are scattered, their food isn't in foreign apps, every plan looks the same, and nobody checks in. CalFit answers each one." (Full version in Chapter 1.)
2. **Who is it for?** Beginners starting out, gym members tracking seriously, and friends motivating each other.
3. **Why one app for both phone types?** "Writing it once for Android and iPhone saved months and killed a whole family of 'works here, broken there' bugs."
4. **Where does user data live?** "In a secure online account — you only ever see your own records. Login details stay in the phone's secure storage."
5. **How does the AI coach work?** "Your question plus your fitness history goes through a secure middleman to the AI service, which replies with advice, workouts or meal plans. The secret keys never sit in the app."
6. **What if there's no network?** "You get a clear message, not a crash. And I made sure of that after it embarrassed me once."
7. **How do you calculate calories remaining?** "Goal minus eaten. It's shown on screen — no hidden magic."
8. **How did you test it?** "Three layers: I read the whole codebase, I wrote an automatic check script I run after every fix — 32 checks, all passing — and I prepared a step-by-step phone test list. Nothing counts as passed until it runs on a real phone."
9. **What actually broke?** "The app crashed on launch without setup values. The AI provider retired our model overnight. The fasting timer broke on reopen. All fixed, all written down."
10. **What's still unfinished?** "Tighter AI access control, full account deletion, better long-history charts, and complete phone testing. It's all listed openly — hiding it would be worse."
11. **What did you do versus the AI tools?** "I defined every requirement and reviewed and tested each change. AI helped me draft and debug faster, but if it broke, it was my call — and my fix."
12. **What did IT add to what you already knew?** "I came in with software experience. The placement added freelancing, client negotiation, pricing and budgeting — a different skill set, and I keep the two separate."
