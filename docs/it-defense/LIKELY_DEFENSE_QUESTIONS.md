# CalFit — Likely Defence Questions (short, honest answers)

1. **What problem did you find in the real world?** People quit fitness within weeks: records scattered, local dishes missing, generic plans, no accountability. CalFit fixes each one — see Chapter 1.
2. **Who is the app for?** Beginners, gym-goers, and friends motivating each other.
3. **Why one app for two phone types?** One codebase serves Android and iPhone, saving time and avoiding double work.
4. **Where is user data kept?** In a secure online account; each user only ever sees their own records. Login details stay in the phone's secure storage.
5. **How does the AI coach work?** The app sends your question plus your fitness history through a secure middleman to the AI service, which replies with advice, workouts or meal plans.
6. **What happens when there is no network?** The app shows a clear message instead of crashing; saved records remain.
7. **How do you calculate calories remaining?** Daily goal minus food eaten. Simple and shown on screen.
8. **How did you test it?** Read the code for weak spots, ran 32 automatic checks (all pass), and prepared a step-by-step phone test list. Nothing is marked passed until run on a real phone.
9. **What broke during testing?** The app crashed on launch without setup values, the AI provider retired a model, and the fasting timer broke after reopening — all fixed and recorded.
10. **What is still unfinished?** Tighter AI access control, full account deletion, chart redesign for long histories, and complete phone testing. All listed openly in the report.
11. **What did YOU do versus the AI tools?** I defined every requirement, chose what to build, and reviewed and tested each change. AI tools helped draft and debug, but I decided what entered the project.
12. **What did you learn during IT?** Freelancing, client negotiation, pricing and budgeting — on top of the software experience I already had.
